Haruyuzuriha is a solo developer brand. Everything here is built by one person, in the open, with the intent of making a genuine craft — one project at a time.

## Philosophy

While the brand name is an aesthetic choice and carry no deep meaning within my scope, one thing stays clear. My effort isn't done to chase money. 
It's done to create, and to find ikigai — a reason to get up and build — in the process. That's the actual measure of success here, before revenue, before users, before anything else: does this project come from something worth making, and does it hold up as work I can stand behind.
 
The tools built under this name are meant to genuinely showcase the love to the craft, while preserving meaning and purpose — not shipped for their own sake, and not shipped to chase a market that doesn't need them.

## Principles across every project

- **Fully local, by default.** No project here depends on infrastructure the maintainer operates.
- **Documentation first.** Every project's design is written down before it's built.
- **One person, honestly.** No claims of a team or a support org that doesn't exist — just clear, upfront statements of what's actually backing each release.
